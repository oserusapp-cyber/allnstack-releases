# FAQ

## Do I need a server?

Yes. The app is where your team works, and the server is what connects everyone and keeps the chats,
the team and the saved logins. Your agency runs one server; everyone’s app signs in to it. Ask your
agency owner for the address.

## Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that safe?

The installer isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows doesn’t recognise the publisher. Click **More info**,
then **Run anyway**. Only download Allnstack from
[the official Releases page](https://github.com/oserusapp-cyber/allnstack-releases/releases/latest).

## How do I update?

You don’t need to. Allnstack downloads new versions in the background and installs them when you quit,
or when you click **Restart to update**.

## I can’t connect to the server

- Check the address with your agency owner, and that you typed it without spaces.
- “Isn’t an Allnstack server” means the address answered but isn’t your agency’s server.
- Check your internet connection, VPN or firewall.

## I forgot my password

Ask your manager or agency owner. They can set a new one for you in **Team**.

## Why can’t I open any accounts?

If your agency uses shifts, accounts open only during your shift (plus a few minutes to finish a
reply). **My work** shows your shift and what you can do on each account.

## Does the AI send messages by itself?

No. AI suggestions are drafts. A person always picks, edits and presses **Send**.

## Is my data shared with anyone?

Your agency’s data lives on your agency’s server. Logins, proxies, AI keys and media are stored
encrypted with your agency’s own key.
