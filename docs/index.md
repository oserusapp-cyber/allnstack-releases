features:
  - title: One inbox for everything
    details: Every account you may work, in one list. The fan who has waited longest is always on top, whichever model they wrote to.
  - title: The right people see the right chats
    details: Owner, admin, manager and chatter roles. Give each chatter exactly the accounts, chats and actions they need. Changes apply at once.
  - title: Shifts that open and close access
    details: Plan the week on a rota. Chatters can open accounts only during their shift, with a short grace period to finish a reply.
  - title: Live teamwork
    details: “Nia is replying…” and “Mo is reading” stop two people answering the same fan.
  - title: AI-assisted replies
    details: Claude drafts replies in your agency’s style, using the fan’s notes and tags. Nothing is sent without a person pressing Send.
  - title: Accounts kept safe
    details: Each model has her own browser identity and proxy, and messages go out at a natural, human pace. Chatters never see passwords.
